Eat, Drink & Play

Casual bites, lakefront rides, easygoing shopping, and skyline fireworks — a little bit of everything, all in one walk along the water.

Find Your Next Favorite Spot

From a quick snack between rides to a sit-down dinner with a lake view, here's what's waiting on the pier.

Restaurants & Bars

Casual counters and sit-down spots like Lakeview Grill and Pier Pizza Co. serve everything from quick bites to full dinners with a water view.

Rides & Attractions

A landmark wheel, a vintage carousel, and a handful of family-friendly rides keep the pier moving from morning to night.

Shopping

Small shops and pop-up stalls along the promenade carry souvenirs, local crafts, and easy gifts to take home.

Fireworks & Light Shows

Seasonal fireworks and light displays turn the pier into the best seat on the lake on select evenings.
