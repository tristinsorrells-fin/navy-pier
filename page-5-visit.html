Visitor Info

General, illustrative guidance for this concept site — always confirm current details before you head out.

Hours & Admission

The pier grounds are open daily, with extended hours in summer. Walking the pier is free; individual rides, cruises, and attractions are ticketed separately.

Parking

On-site parking garages and nearby lots serve the pier, with discounted rates available when you validate at select shops and attractions.

Accessibility

Ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms are available throughout the grounds, and wheelchairs can be reserved for the day at the welcome desk.

Tickets & Pier Passport

Buy individual ride and cruise tickets, or bundle a handful of favorites into a single Pier Passport for a discount.
