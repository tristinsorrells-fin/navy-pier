What's On

A rotating lineup of free and ticketed events runs on the pier all year — here are a few of the seasonal favorites.

Seasonal Events

Dates are illustrative for this concept site — check back for the full lineup each season.

Jun–AugWeekly

Summer Concert Series

Local and touring bands play free outdoor sets on the pier's main stage all summer long.

Wed & SatSummer

Fireworks Series

Twice-weekly fireworks light up the lake, best viewed from the end of the pier.

Nov–JanSeasonal

Winterfest

Ice sculptures, holiday lights, and a cozy warm-up lounge take over the pier through winter.

SeptAnnual

Art on the Pier

A juried outdoor art fair fills the promenade with painting, sculpture, and craft vendors.

SatYear-Round

Family Storytime

A weekend morning storytelling hour for young visitors, hosted near the indoor garden.
